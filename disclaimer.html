SiteEzy Disclaimer & Terms

This page explains, in plain language, exactly what a SiteEzy package includes, what it doesn't, and where extra work is billed separately. It isn't formal legal advice — just a clear, honest explanation so there are no surprises.

What's Included

  • Package pricing covers only the features listed for that package — nothing more, nothing hidden.

Your Content & Domain

  • Domain name registration and yearly renewal are separate costs, paid by you to the domain registrar.
  • You provide your business content — text, photos, product information, prices, logos, and any other material needed to build your site, unless a separate content-entry service is agreed.

Revisions

  • Each package includes a set number of revision rounds — one complete, consolidated list of changes, sent together, not ongoing back-and-forth.
  • ₹5,000 MINI — 1 round of revisions before final delivery.
  • ₹10,000 BUSINESS — 2 rounds of revisions.
  • ₹15,000 CATALOGUE — 2 rounds of revisions, plus one pass to correct your initial product listings (typos, prices, photos).
  • ₹18,000 FLAGSHIP — 3 rounds of revisions.
  • Within your included rounds you can adjust text, swap photos, and fix errors. Adding entirely new sections, pages or features counts as separate work, quoted upfront.

Marketing & SEO

  • Basic on-page SEO is good practice, not a guarantee — it does not guarantee search rankings, traffic, leads or sales.

Third-Party Services

  • Third-party services (domains, hosting, WhatsApp, Google Maps, etc.) have their own policies, pricing and availability, which SiteEzy does not control.

Delivery

  • Delivery timelines depend on receiving your content and approvals on time.
  • Changes requested after final delivery that fall outside the originally agreed scope may involve an additional cost.
  • We hand over your finished website files together with a simple written guide for publishing them and connecting your domain. Hands-on setup help is available separately, on request.

What's Not Included

  • Advanced systems — bookings, logins, payments, custom software — are not automatically included in any package.
  • The ₹15,000 / ₹18,000 catalogue + cart + WhatsApp ordering feature is not a full e-commerce platform.
  • Payment gateways are not included unless specifically discussed and agreed separately.
  • Ongoing maintenance and content updates after delivery are not included unless agreed separately — SiteEzy is not a monthly retainer service by default.
  • The ₹15,000 CATALOGUE package includes up to 20 products. If a catalogue is part of an agreed ₹18,000 FLAGSHIP project, it includes up to 30. Extra products beyond your package's limit can be added afterwards in paid batches.

Ownership & Approval

  • Any photos, logos or content you provide remain your property at all times.
  • You should review your website carefully before giving final approval for it to go live.

Extra & Custom Work

  • Requirements outside a standard package (custom features, integrations, etc.) require their own separate quotation.
