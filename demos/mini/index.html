About Us

Small café, big comfort.

Pepper Leaf Café is a small neighbourhood spot in Kottayam built around one idea: good coffee shouldn't be complicated. We keep our menu short, our ingredients simple, and our tables unhurried.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick filter coffee or settling in with a book for the afternoon, our rustic little corner is set up to make you feel at home.
