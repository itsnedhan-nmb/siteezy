## Why the 5 `_review` images were set aside

- **Real, readable business names/signage** that don't match our fictional businesses (`Classic Cafe`, `THE YARD GYM` ×2, `SNYX`) — using these would misrepresent a real company's branding as if it were the demo business's own, and would visually contradict the business names used elsewhere on the page.
- **Identifiable real people** in one gym photo who have no connection to the fictional Nova Fitness Studio and never consented to appearing on this site.
- **One content mismatch** (fried chicken combo meal) that doesn't fit Pepper Leaf Café's stated simple café/filter-coffee/dosa menu.

None of these were deleted — they're sitting in `assets/_review/` if you want to look at them yourself and decide otherwise (for example, the signage ones could still be used if cropped/blurred to hide the readable text).
